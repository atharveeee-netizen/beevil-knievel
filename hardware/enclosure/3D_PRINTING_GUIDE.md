# Beevil Knievel — 3D Printable Enclosure Manufacturing Guide

---

## 🖨️ 3D Printing Specifications (Hive Node Body)

| Parameter | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Filament Material** | **PETG or ASA** | High UV resistance, heat tolerance (up to 85°C), weather-proof |
| **Infill Density** | **40% Gyroid Infill** | Maximum mechanical strength against outdoor wind/impact |
| **Layer Height** | **0.20 mm** | Optimum balance between print speed and layer adhesion |
| **Wall Loops / Perimeters** | **4 Perimeters** | Ensures 100% water-tight outer shell |
| **Print Bed Temp** | 75°C (PETG) / 105°C (ASA) | Prevents warping during print |
| **Print Speed** | 50 mm/s | Ensures precision around micro-O-ring gasket grooves |

---

## 🌧️ IP67 Waterproofing & Weatherization

1. **Top Lid Gasket**: Place a 1.5mm silicone O-ring gasket in the perimeter channel before screwing the lid cap down.
2. **ICS-43434 Mic Vent**: Cover the 6mm bottom audio port with a **Gore-Tex IP67 acoustic vent membrane** (Part # ePTFE-VENT-06) to allow sound frequencies (200-400Hz) through while blocking water droplets and dust.
3. **Propolis Seal Protection**: Apply a thin coat of clear conformal coating spray to the PCB solder joints to prevent bees from depositing sticky propolis inside the enclosure.

---

## 🐝 Langstroth Hive Box Mounting

* **Mounting Type**: Non-Invasive Stainless Steel Strap Mount.
* **Position**: Mount node on the **North or East-facing outer wall** of the hive brood box (reduces direct intense midday heat radiation while allowing top solar panel to capture ambient light).
