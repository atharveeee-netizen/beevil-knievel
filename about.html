Our Mission

Protecting the foundation of agriculture.

We are building industrial-grade intelligence tools to prevent colony collapse and secure global food supply chains through precision apiculture.

Our Story

Beevil Knievel originated out of the School of Applied Sciences, Engineering & Technology (SASET) at Rashtriya Raksha University (RRU) in Gujarat, India.

Recognizing that manual hive inspections were inherently reactive, our founding engineering team sought to apply modern Edge AI and Sub-GHz telecommunications to create a proactive, continuous monitoring system.

Today, Beevil Knievel operates as a commercial hardware startup, iterating on robust, IP68-weatherproof sensor nodes designed for field deployment at scale.
