Component Details

  • TMP117: High-precision NIST-traceable temperature sensor. Measures brood nest thermal fluctuations with ±0.08°C calibrated accuracy.
  • SHT45: Low-power relative humidity sensor (±1.5% RH accuracy). Detects condensation and fungal disease risks (Chalkbrood).
  • BME688: AI-enabled gas sensor. Measures volatile organic compound (VOC) resistance ($k\Omega$) to detect bacterial pathogens (American Foulbrood).
  • ICS-43434: Low-noise 24-bit digital I2S MEMS microphone. Captures 20Hz–20kHz acoustic signals for spectral analysis.
  • LIS2DW12: 3-axis accelerometer (50 nA sleep). Detects comb structural vibration and triggers wake-on-motion interrupts during tamper.
  • OPT3001: Ambient light sensor. Detects lid-open hive inspections and theft.
  • nRF52840 MCU: 64 MHz ARM Cortex-M4F with hardware Floating Point Unit (FPU). Runs FreeRTOS, CMSIS-DSP, and TFLite Micro.
  • SX1262 LoRa Transceiver: Sub-GHz Class A radio transmitting AES-128 encrypted packets up to 5.4 km.
  • BQ25570 MPPT Harvester: Extracts maximum power from a 50×35 mm solar panel, keeping battery charged continuously.
