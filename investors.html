The Market Opportunity

A third of the global diet is dependent on honeybee pollination. The commercial apiculture market suffers from severe inefficiencies, with an average 40% annual colony loss rate in the US alone.

By replacing manual, labor-intensive apiary inspections with autonomous sensor nodes, we estimate a TAM (Total Addressable Market) of over $4.2 Billion in global crop pollination protection and commercial honey production.
