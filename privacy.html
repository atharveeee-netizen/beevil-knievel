Legal & Privacy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Security & Encryption

All sensor telemetry transmitted over Sub-GHz LoRaWAN is protected with AES-128 end-to-end encryption. Data uploaded to the cloud diagnostic engine is stored in encrypted databases complying with ISO 27001 standard frameworks. We do not sell, rent, or trade your apiary location data, colony health records, or harvest yield metrics to third parties.

2. Commercial Terms of Sale

Pre-orders for Beevil Knievel hardware kits are subject to a 30-day money-back guarantee. Standard hardware packages come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal outdoor apiary operation.

3. GDPR & Data Deletion

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users maintain the right to request full deletion of their account and associated apiary telemetry data. Contact our legal privacy officer at privacy@beevilknievel.com to initiate a data purge.

4. Progressive Web App (PWA) Storage

The Beevil Knievel Farmer App uses local IndexedDB and Service Worker caching to store sensor telemetry locally on your phone. This ensures you can access your latest synced data when offline in the field. This data is kept strictly on your local device.
