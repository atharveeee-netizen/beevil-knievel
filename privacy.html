Privacy Policy & Commercial Terms

1. Data Security & Encryption

All sensor telemetry transmitted over Sub-GHz LoRaWAN is protected with AES-128 end-to-end encryption. Data uploaded to the cloud diagnostic engine is stored in encrypted databases complying with ISO 27001 standards.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your apiary location data, colony health records, or harvest yield metrics to third parties.

2. Commercial Terms of Sale

Pre-orders for Beevil Knievel hardware kits are subject to a 30-day money-back guarantee. Standard hardware packages come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal outdoor apiary operation.

3. Progressive Web App (PWA) Storage

The Beevil Knievel Farmer App uses local IndexedDB and Service Worker caching to store sensor telemetry locally when your device is offline in the field.
