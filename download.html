Installation Guide

Step-by-Step Setup

Android Installation (APK)

  1. Tap Download Android APK button above.
  2. Open your Downloads folder and select `Beevil_v2.4.0.apk`.
  3. If prompted, enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in Android Settings.
  4. Launch the app and log in with your apiary account credentials.

iOS & Desktop Installation (PWA)

  1. Open this website in Safari on iOS or Chrome on Desktop.
  2. Tap the Share icon (iOS) or browser menu (Chrome).
  3. Select Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch the app icon directly from your home screen for full offline access.
