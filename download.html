Setting Up on Your Phone

Download the APK

Step 02
Allow Unknown Apps

If prompted by Android, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser in system settings.

Pair Your Gateway

Open the app and type in your Wio-E5 gateway ID to connect your hive sensors.
