Hardware Mounting & Placement

Proper installation is critical for accurate thermal readings and solar battery maintenance.

1. Mount the IP68 weatherproof enclosure directly on the exterior side wall of the brood chamber using the included stainless steel bracket.
2. Ensure the solar top panel faces south for optimal trickle-charging.
3. Insert Probe A into the central brood nest frame (between frames 4 and 5) to monitor core temperature.
4. Place Probe B under the outer hive lid to capture ambient reference temperature.

Wio-E5 LoRa Gateway Setup

Power on the Gateway station. Connect it to your local Wi-Fi or cellular backhaul. Pair your sensor node using the unit EUI printed on the hardware label via the AT command interface:
