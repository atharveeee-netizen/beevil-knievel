Technical User Guide & Specs

Documentation & Field Guide

1. Hardware Mounting & Placement

Mount the IP68 weatherproof enclosure directly on the exterior side wall of the brood chamber using the included stainless steel bracket. Ensure the solar top panel faces south for optimal trickle-charging.

Insert Probe A into the central brood nest frame (between frames 4 and 5) to monitor core temperature. Place Probe B under the outer hive lid to capture ambient reference temperature.

2. Wio-E5 LoRa Gateway Setup

Power on the Gateway station. Connect it to your local Wi-Fi or insertion of a 4G SIM card. Pair your sensor node using the unit EUI printed on the hardware label:

# Example Gateway Pairing Command (AT Command Interface)
AT+KEY=APPKEY,"2B7E151628AED2A6ABF7158809CF4F3C"
AT+JOIN=AUTO

3. AI Thresholds & Diagnostic Rule Engine

Swarming Detection: Triggers when Brood Core Temp spikes +3.0°C above baseline while 200–400Hz acoustic FFT energy exceeds 82dB for over 15 minutes.

Varroa Mite Attack: Triggers when Brood Core Temp drops below 32.5°C while high-frequency agitation hums (400–450Hz) are detected.
