# DESIGN SYSTEM SPECIFICATION (Design.md)

**Project:** BEEVIL KNIEVEL  
**Domain:** Engineering Visual Design & Dashboard Interface  

---

## 1. Design Token System

### 1.1 Color Hierarchy (Industrial Technical Theme)
- **Matte Base**: `#070a14` (Deep obsidian darkroom background)
- **Container Slate**: `#0f172a` (Card base and group enclosure)
- **Border Active**: `#334155` (Subtle boundary), `#475569` (Active border)
- **Telemetry Cyan**: `#38bdf8` (High-speed acoustic data and I2S)
- **Biological Amber**: `#fbbf24` (Thermoregulation, brood cluster, swarm alert)
- **Nominal Emerald**: `#34d399` (Validated state, healthy queen, power nominal)
- **Alarm Rose**: `#fb7185` (Critical temperature drop, foulbrood alert)
- **Radio Violet**: `#a855f7` (Sub-GHz LoRa RF transmission link)

### 1.2 Typography
- **Headlines**: Semi-expanded, geometric grotesque sans-serif (`system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `Roboto`).
- **Telemetry Readouts**: Tabular figures monospace (`"SF Mono"`, `"JetBrains Mono"`, `Consolas`).
- **Letter Spacing**: +0.5px on technical badges, +0.8px on all-caps titles.

---

## 2. Information Density
- Maximum data ink ratio (Tufte principle).
- No superfluous drop-shadows, blurred glows, or faux-3D embellishments.
- All diagrams and charts maintain high contrast legible on both outdoor sunlight-readable displays and darkroom operational monitors.
