Hardware & Engineering

Engineered for extreme apiary conditions

Commercial beekeeping demands hardware that operates autonomously in remote locations without maintenance for years.

Hardware Architecture

Wio-E5 / STM32WLE5 SoC

Built on an ultra-low-power ARM Cortex-M4 microcontroller running on-device Model 1 TinyML signal analysis in 8.4ms. Mounts directly to the hive wall.

Diagnostic AI

Model 2 Cloud AI Engine

Cross-references multi-day thermal delta-T (ΔT) and 200–400Hz acoustic spectrums to accurately diagnose swarming, queen loss, and Varroa mite attacks.

Alert Gateway

Direct Mobile Notifications

Dispatches instant WhatsApp and SMS pings to the farmer's phone when a hive needs urgent field inspection, backed by full offline caching.
