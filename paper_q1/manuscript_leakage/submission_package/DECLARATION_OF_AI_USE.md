# Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted Technologies in the Writing Process

**Manuscript Title:** Split Protocol or Feature Transform? Explaining Generalisation Gaps in Acoustic Honeybee Colony Classification  
**Journal:** *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* (Elsevier)

During the preparation of this work, the authors used large language model assistants (specifically Google DeepMind Gemini and Antigravity IDE) in order to assist with:
1. Automated LaTeX macro typesetting and compilation scripts.
2. Code formatting, documentation, and unit test scaffold generation.
3. Grammar and stylistic proofreading of author-written prose.

After using these tools, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the published article. All experimental designs, data analyses, statistical calculations, scientific conclusions, and final interpretations were conceived, verified, and approved exclusively by the authors.
